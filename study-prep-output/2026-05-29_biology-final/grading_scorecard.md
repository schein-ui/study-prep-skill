## Six grading criteria

### 1. Content Accuracy — **A−**

Strengths:
- All content drawn from the actual teacher slides and the teacher-distributed review guide. 27 of 28 Drive files were ingested.
- Teacher exclusion (Hardy-Weinberg) explicitly captured and honored across all deliverables.
- Worked examples (DNA → mRNA → amino acid; 9:3:3:1 dihybrid; AB × O blood type cross; carrier × normal X-linked) all verified against the worksheet answer keys.

Honest deduction (−):
- No image rendering of slide PDFs — I worked from text-extracted slide content only. If a slide had a specific figure the teacher emphasizes verbally in class (e.g., a specific cladogram, a specific labeled karyotype), I described the concept but didn't visually verify the exact image.
- One slight imprecision: oogenesis is sometimes taught as "1 ovum + 2 polar bodies" (if the first polar body doesn't divide) and sometimes as "1 ovum + 3 polar bodies" (if it does). I used "1 egg + 3 polar bodies" matching the slide deck Chloe was taught from — but worth flagging in case the test phrases it differently.

### 2. Curriculum Fit — **A**

Strengths:
- Every topic in the teacher's review guide is covered, in the same order.
- Every vocab word in the teacher's review guide is in the glossary.
- Every "be able to..." question in the review guide has a matching answer somewhere in the bundle.
- 9th-grade language register throughout (no college-level jargon; no oversimplifying to middle-school level).

No deduction — this is the strongest dimension.

### 3. Pedagogy — **B+**

Strengths:
- Refutation-text approach used for the most common confusions ("Watch Out" callouts pair the WRONG version with the RIGHT version).
- Active recall built in via the `<details>` click-to-reveal Check-Yourself questions inside the HTML guide.
- The full quiz bank (60+ items) supports retrieval practice across multiple days.
- The study plan is spaced (13 days, topic per day) rather than crammed.
- Flashcards for spaced repetition on phone.

Honest deductions (B+ not A):
- **No personalized "Watch Out" cards from Chloe's actual errors.** This is the biggest pedagogy miss — the skill identifies the student-work audit as the single highest-leverage move, and we couldn't do it (no completed homework in the Drive folder). The general "Watch Out" cards are based on classic teen-bio confusions, not on Chloe's specific patterns.
- The interactive features (click-to-reveal) only work in the HTML version. In the printed PDF, they print expanded — fine for studying, but loses the active-recall benefit.

**This grade should be re-evaluated as A− or B if a follow-up run can include the audit.** Without it, the bundle is doing solid generic pedagogy, not personalized pedagogy.

### 4. Assessment Quality — **A−**

Strengths:
- Quiz bank spans MC, short-answer, "show your work" Punnett squares, sequence-tracing (blood flow, O₂ path), and 3 cross-topic synthesis questions.
- Each item has a worked answer with reasoning, not just a letter.
- Distractor design on MC items is plausible (e.g., the dependent variable item — "plant heights are the:" — uses real misconception traps).
- Coverage matches the review guide's emphasis (lots of genetics, evolution, ecology, human body; less science method).

Honest deduction (−):
- No "diagram-drawing" assessment items (e.g., "draw and label the heart" or "draw the menstrual cycle hormone graph"). The teacher's review guide says "Be able to label the following parts of the human heart..." — that's a drawing/labeling item type that's not directly practiced in the quiz bank. Mitigated somewhat by the diagram PDFs (Chloe can use those to self-quiz on labeling), but not as a graded item.

### 5. Engagement — **A−**

Strengths:
- Direct-address ("Chloe", "you've got this") in README and footer.
- Color-coded callouts (orange Watch Out, green Tip, red Exclusion) — easy to scan.
- Standalone HTML works on phone — fits how a teen actually studies (in bed, on the bus).
- SVG diagrams in green/blue palette to match study guide aesthetic, not chalkboard-yellow.
- Cheat sheet is intentionally dense + visually packed (the goal is "scan it all in 15 minutes").
- Print pack is one document, staple-ready.

Honest deductions (−):
- No memorable mnemonics inserted (e.g., "King Philip Came Over For Green Spaghetti" for taxonomy is mentioned but not as a featured callout). The Evolution of Populations slides flagged this mnemonic and we relayed it but didn't elevate it.
- Could use more analogies / real-life hooks for the abstract material (e.g., enzymes-as-keys, RNA-as-blueprint-photocopy).

### 6. Cross-Deliverable Consistency — **A**

Strengths:
- Topic order matches across study plan, study guide, glossary, quiz bank, cheat sheet, and flashcards.
- "Hardy-Weinberg excluded" appears consistently in plan, guide, README, and QC report.
- Test date "Friday May 29, 2026 · 1–3 PM · North Gym" appears identically in every artifact.
- "Watch Out: pulmonary artery carries DEOXYGENATED blood" cross-references between study guide, cheat sheet, and quiz Q60.
- Vocabulary terms are defined identically in glossary, study guide body, and cheat sheet.

No deduction.
