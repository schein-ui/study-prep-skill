## TODO: Day 1 — Comprehension + Glossary [~2 hrs]

**Goal:** See the whole unit. Don't memorize yet.

1. **(15 min)** Read "⚠ Errors in My Notes" FIRST. Fix the <N> flagged mistakes before anything else.
2. **(30 min)** Read through the interactive study guide.
3. **(30 min)** Glossary pass — read once; mark 8-10 slippery terms.
4. **(15 min)** Look at all SVG diagrams. Cover labels, try to name each.
5. **(15-30 min)** 5 random MC questions from the quiz bank.

---

## TODO: Day 2 — Active Recall + Quiz Bank [~2.5 hrs]

**Goal:** Find out what you don't know and fix it.

1. **(20 min)** Cold retrieval — write down from memory: <subject-specific checklist>.
2. **(60 min)** All MC + SA. Grade yourself.
3. **(20 min)** Re-read sections you missed. Focus on WHY wrong answers are wrong.
4. **(30 min)** Flashcards in Learn mode. Must-Know first.

---

## TODO: Day 3 — Diagrams + FRQ + Error Review [~1.5 hrs]

**Goal:** Lock in visual and writing questions.

1. **(15 min)** Re-read errors. Say correct version out loud for each.
2. **(30 min)** All diagram questions.
3. **(30 min)** All FRQs, timed, in full sentences.
4. **(15 min)** Flashcard round 2 — cards missed yesterday.
5. **(15 min max)** Skim cheat sheet. BED EARLY.

---

## TODO: Test morning — Cheat sheet scan only [15 min max]

1. Cheat sheet front to back, once.
2. Say out loud: <top 3-5 must-remember items>.
3. **STOP.** Eat breakfast. Trust what you've built.

---

## Rules

- Retrieval > re-reading. Quizzing feels harder but works better.
- Interleave — mix chapters inside a session.
- Sleep > last hour of studying.
- Don't study past 9pm the night before.
